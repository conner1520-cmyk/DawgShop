## 90-second pitch (about 210 words)

> **[0:00, hook]** Show of hands: who's bought or sold something in the UGA Free & For Sale group? Who's been ghosted, or scammed, or watched a perfectly good couch go to the curb in May?
>
> **[0:15, problem]** Students buy and sell constantly, but the tools are Facebook groups and GroupMes. You don't know who's on the other end, posts get buried in an hour, and seniors moving out in May never connect with freshmen moving in in August.
>
> **[0:30, solution]** DawgShop is a marketplace for UGA students only. Every account is a verified @uga.edu email, so you know you're dealing with another Dawg.
>
> **[0:40, demo; show your phone or laptop]** Here's Browse: everything for sale near campus. I can filter by my dorm instead of a map radius. Posting takes under a minute: photos, price, pickup spot. And this is the part I'm proudest of: **move-in listings**. A senior lists a desk now, sets it available August 10th, and an incoming freshman can find it on the Move-in tab.
>
> **[1:05, proof]** It's live right now. I built and deployed it in about six weeks using AI coding agents, and I'm not a developer.
>
> **[1:15, ask]** Next up are in-app messaging and AI that writes your listing from a photo. Come to my table, sign up with your UGA email, and post the first thing you want to get rid of.

## Table demo: 3-minute walkthrough

1. **Browse**: scroll the feed, search a course number (for example "MATH 2250"), then tap a category chip.
2. **Filter**: open the filter sheet and pick a pickup spot and a max price.
3. **A listing**: swipe through the photos and point out the seller's name and pickup spot. The email is never shown.
4. **Move-in tab**: listings grouped by month.
5. **Sell**: walk through posting (you can stop before posting, or post and then delete it).
6. **Me tab**: Edit, Mark sold, Unlist. Mark one sold and show it disappear from Browse.
7. **Hand them your phone**, or have them sign up on theirs if they have a @uga.edu email.

**Likely questions:**
- *Why not just use Facebook?* Verified students only, dorm-based pickup and move-in timing. Facebook can't do any of the three.
- *How do people pay?* In person, with cash or Venmo. No payments in the app means nothing to hold and no fees.
- *How do you get the first users?* Launch in one dorm or club, seed it with my own and friends' listings, and time it to December and May move-outs.
- *How does it make money?* Not yet. The options are featured listings, ads from Athens businesses (movers, storage, furniture) and summer storage.
- *What was hardest?* UGA's email security scanner "clicked" every sign-in link before students could, so I switched to typed codes.
